## 1. Year-End Wrapped Synthesis

Analyze year-end "Wrapped" outputs from multiple platforms to infer a fun, playful, and expressive personal persona. Generate one high-fidelity image prompt that reflects personality with energy, charm, and visual delight, while staying evidence-based. The goal is to create a unified wrapped that synthesizes all platform-specific wrapped outputs, which the user can then use to generate a persona image.

Model: Claude/GPT-4 (Best)

**Prompt:**

> **Role:** Act as a synthesis engine that analyzes year-end "Wrapped" outputs from multiple platforms. Your goal is to infer a fun, playful, and expressive personal persona from these Wrappeds and generate one high-fidelity image prompt that reflects the personality with energy, charm, and visual delight, while staying evidence-based.
> 
> **Tone:** The tone should feel human, modern, curious, and joyful, not serious or corporate.
> 
> **Inputs:**
> 
> I will upload:
> 
> - Screenshots, images, or short videos of official "Wrapped" outputs from different platforms.
> - These may include stats, themes, visuals, captions, emojis, or color styles.
> 
> Do not assume missing data. Use only what is visible or clearly implied.
> 
> **Step 1: Extract Signals (Do Not Skip)**
> 
> From each Wrapped, extract:
> 
> - Playfulness signals (humor, experimentation, whimsy, spontaneity)
> - Taste signals (genres, creators, aesthetics, visual styles)
> - Energy signals (high-tempo vs chill, expressive vs introspective)
> - Social signals (sharing, community, creation vs consumption)
> - Visual language cues (colors, icons, emojis, motion)
> 
> List signals per platform before synthesizing.
> 
> **Step 2: Synthesize My Fun Persona**
> 
> Create a persona that feels approachable and lively, not performative.
> 
> A. **Persona Title:** A playful 2–4 word archetype (e.g., "Curious Chaos", "Joyful Builder", "Playful Optimizer").
> 
> B. **Core Traits (5 bullets max):** Traits should reflect fun through action, not adjectives alone (e.g., "plays with structure," "alternates focus with escape").
> 
> C. **Motivations & Joy Triggers:** What excites me, energizes me, or keeps me engaged.
> 
> D. **Contrasts & Quirks:** Where seriousness meets fun (e.g., disciplined but goofy).
> 
> E. **One-Sentence Identity Statement:** Light, confident, and memorable—something I'd actually post.
> 
> Avoid clichés. Avoid irony. Avoid forced humor.
> 
> **Step 3: Translate Persona → Fun Visual Language**
> 
> Convert the persona into a playful visual identity:
> 
> - Mood: joyful, curious, mischievous, warm, or energetic
> - Color palette: bright accents, unexpected combinations, or soft vibrancy
> - Lighting: natural glow, golden hour, colorful highlights, dynamic shadows
> - Environment: creative spaces, movement-friendly settings, light chaos
> - Clothing: expressive details, texture, relaxed confidence
> - Body language: motion, asymmetry, candid moments
> - Camera framing: dynamic angles, off-center composition, depth
> - Style: modern, cinematic-playful, editorial-casual, or stylized realism
> 
> Briefly justify choices.
> 
> **Step 4: Generate The Image Prompt**
> 
> Produce one final image-generation prompt that:
> 
> - Captures fun without being cartoonish
> - Feels like a candid, joyful version of me
> - Reflects energy, curiosity, and warmth
> - Would make someone say: "That feels like you."
> 
> **Prompt Rules:**
> 
> - Single clean prompt
> - No placeholders
> - No explanations inside the prompt
> - Include: mood, colors, lighting, environment, pose, framing, style
> - No platform logos or app references
> 
> **Constraints:**
> 
> - Fun must emerge from behavior and taste, not exaggeration.
> - Do not invent traits or life events.
> - If signals conflict, express them through playful contrast.
> - Image should feel alive, not posed or stiff.
> 
> **Required Output:**
> 
> 1. Fun Persona Summary
> 2. Playful Visual Translation
> 3. Final Image Prompt (copy-paste ready)
